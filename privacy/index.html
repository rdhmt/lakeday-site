Last updated: November 18, 2026

LakeDay Ventures builds and operates internal software tools. These tools are used privately by LakeDay and people it authorizes. They are not offered to the public.

Information we access. When a user connects an account, our tools access only the data that user explicitly authorizes, such as email or calendar data, and only for the purposes described below.

How we use it. Data is used solely to provide features to the user who authorized access. We do not sell data, use it for advertising or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Sharing. Data is processed on systems LakeDay controls and by service providers that help us operate our tools, under their terms. We do not share data with anyone else unless required by law.

Security and retention. We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service and protect access credentials with restricted storage.

Your choices. You can revoke access at any time. For Google accounts, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
